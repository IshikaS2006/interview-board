Store strokes as immutable events instead of current state
Enables time-travel debugging, undo/redo
Impact: Better data consistency & audit trail

Impact: Massive performance boost for old rooms

Async processing for DB writes, notifications
Decouple real-time updates from persistence
Impact: Better resilience & performance

Impact: Handle 10x more concurrent users
